    On your other computer: install Password Vault, choose “Already use Password Vault on another computer? Connect to it”, pick the same cloud drive, and unlock with this master password.
    Change master password

    For your security, changing your master password always needs the current one typed in — Touch ID and Windows Hello can't be used here.

    Your vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your master password (scrypt). It is stored only on this computer and locks automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Copied passwords are cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
